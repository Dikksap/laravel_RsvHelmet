Children are organised by business domain (catalog_resources, inventory_resources, production_resources, external_sync_resources, item_requests_resource) and share cross-cutting concerns in filament_shared_assets (Auth/Login, Exports/Imports, Forms.Components.InfiniteSelect, Widgets, base Page classes like InfiniteScrollListRecords). Domain Resources register their CRUD Pages, tables, forms, and RelationManagers; shared assets provide reusable building blocks — the InfiniteSelect form component, export/import definitions mirroring each resource's model, dashboard widgets, and base Page classes. Blade templates under resources/views/filament/pages render the custom Filament Pages (BOM builder, stock card, warehouse, batch realization, etc.), while partials supply the branded auth panel and SPA infinite-scroll fix. Wiring into Filament is done via standard service-provider registration of Resources, Pages, Widgets, and Form Components rather than a central bootstrap file.
