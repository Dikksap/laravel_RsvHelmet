Two-layer leaf under `app/` with a strict one-way dependency: Services depend on Models; Models never reference Services.

- `app/Models/` — Eloquent entities grouped by domain area: item master (`Item`, `ItemType`, `ItemCategory`, `Brand`, `Uom`, `Division`, `ItemAttribute*`) plus the variant/component graph (`ItemComponent`, `ItemVariantMapping`); inventory ledger (`InventoryMovement`, `InventoryBalance`); production workflow (`ProductionBatch`, `ProductionBatchItem`, `ProductionBatchMaterial`); stocktake (`StockOpname`, `StockOpnameLine`); cross-reference to the external system (`ExternalItemMapping`, `ExternalSyncedBarang`); shared `Warehouse` and `User`. Relationships are declared as typed Eloquent relations (`BelongsTo`, `HasMany`, `BelongsToMany`) and query filters live in `scopeXxx(Builder $query)` methods (`scopeActive`, `scopeParents`, `scopeVariants`, `scopeIncoming`, `scopeOutgoing`).
- `app/Services/` — stateless business services composing model interactions. `StockMovementService` is the central coordinator for all stock changes (`record`, `transfer`, `transferMany`, `recordProductionOutput`, `delete`) and enforces negative-balance prevention via `applyDelta` using `->lockForUpdate()->first()` on `InventoryBalance`. `BomExplosionService::rawMaterials` recursively explodes a `ProductionBatch` through `ItemComponent` edges into raw-material requirements against `InventoryBalance`. `ExternalBarangApiService` abstracts HTTP calls to `/api/public/barang*` (list, detail, rekap-variant, ping) behind a typed interface; `ExternalBarangSyncService` orchestrates the API-driven sync pipeline, idempotently recording movements via `StockMovementService` and persisting per-unit records in `ExternalSyncedBarang` keyed by `kode_barang`. Other services (`BatchProgressService`, `BatchRequirementService`, `ItemRequestService`, `StockOpnameService`) follow the same pattern of composing model queries inside transactional boundaries.

Cross-service composition happens via constructor injection (e.g. `ExternalBarangSyncService` depends on `ExternalBarangApiService` and `StockMovementService`). All mutating operations go through `DB::transaction()` closures, and validation failures surface as `ValidationException` rather than exceptions.
