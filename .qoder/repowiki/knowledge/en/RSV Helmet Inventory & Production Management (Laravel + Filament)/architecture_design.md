Standard Laravel 13 application layered around three runtime concerns:
- Domain layer (`app/Models/`) defines Eloquent entities for items, warehouses, inventory balances/movements, production batches, stock opnames, item requests, UOMs, categories/types/brands/divisions, attributes, and external-sync mappings; relationships model the BOM tree via `ItemComponent` (parent → child) and variant mapping through a pivot table.
- Presentation layer is split between Filament v5 resources/pages/widgets under `app/Filament/` (auto-discovered by `AdminPanelProvider`) and Blade/Livewire views under `resources/views/`; custom Filament components live in `Forms/Components` (e.g. `InfiniteSelect`) and `Pages/` (BOM builder, stock cards, warehouse pages).
- Business logic is extracted into stateless services under `app/Services/` (`BomExplosionService`, `BatchRequirementService`, `StockMovementService`, `StockOpnameService`, `ExternalBarangApiService`, etc.) that models and repositories do not contain directly — controllers and Filament pages delegate to them.
- Data access is centralized in Eloquent models plus database migrations (`database/migrations/`) with factories and seeders (`MasterDataSeeder`, `FinishGoodItemSeeder`, `MasterItemSeeder`) for initial data.
- External integration flows: a console command (`SyncExternalBarangCommand`) drives `ExternalBarangSyncService` which calls `ExternalBarangApiService` and persists results into `ExternalSyncedBarang` / `ExternalItemMapping`; CSV imports/exports are wired through Filament's `Imports/` and `Exports/` classes.
- Deployment uses Docker Compose (MariaDB 11 + app container on port 8001), a separate `Dockerfile.prod` with nginx/php-fpm, and GitHub Actions CI (`github/workflows/ci-cd.yml`).
