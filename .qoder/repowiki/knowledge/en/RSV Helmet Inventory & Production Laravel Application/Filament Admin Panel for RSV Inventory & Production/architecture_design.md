Children are split by domain (catalog_resources, inventory_resources, production_resources, external_sync_resources, item_requests_resource) plus shared cross-cutting concerns in filament_shared_assets (Auth/Login, Exports/Imports, Forms.Components.InfiniteSelect, Widgets, Pages base classes) and presentation in filament_views. Domain Resources register their CRUD pages, tables, forms, and RelationManagers; shared assets provide reusable building blocks — a custom InfiniteSelect form component, export/import definitions mirroring each resource's model, dashboard widgets, and base Page classes like InfiniteScrollListRecords. Blade templates under resources/views/filament/pages render the custom Filament Pages (BOM builder, stock card, warehouse, etc.), while partials supply the branded auth panel and SPA infinite-scroll fix. The module is wired into Filament via standard service-provider registration of Resources, Pages, Widgets, and Form Components rather than a central bootstrap file.
