Two-layer leaf module under `app/`:
- `app/Models/` — Eloquent entities representing the domain: item catalog (`Item`, `ItemType`, `ItemCategory`, `Brand`, `Uom`, `ItemAttribute*`), variant/component graph (`ItemComponent`, `ItemVariantMapping`), inventory ledger (`InventoryMovement`, `InventoryBalance`), production workflow (`ProductionBatch`, `ProductionBatchItem`, `ProductionBatchMaterial`), stocktake (`StockOpname`, `StockOpnameLine`), cross-reference (`ExternalItemMapping`, `ExternalSyncedBarang`), plus shared `Warehouse` and `User`. Relationships are declared as typed Eloquent relations (`BelongsTo`, `HasMany`, `BelongsToMany`) with scopes like `scopeActive`, `scopeParents`, `scopeVariants`, and accessors for labels/computed values.
- `app/Services/` — stateless business services that orchestrate model interactions. `StockMovementService` is the central coordinator for all stock changes (record, transfer, transferMany, recordProductionOutput, delete) and enforces negative-balance prevention via `applyDelta` using `lockForUpdate` on `InventoryBalance`. `BomExplosionService` recursively explodes a `ProductionBatch` through `ItemComponent` edges into raw-material requirements. `ExternalBarangSyncService` orchestrates an API-driven sync pipeline against `ExternalBarangApiService`, idempotently recording movements and mapping variants. Other services (`BatchProgressService`, `BatchRequirementService`, `ItemRequestService`, `StockOpnameService`) follow the same pattern of composing model queries inside transactional boundaries.

Dependency direction is one-way: Services depend on Models; Models do not reference Services. Cross-service composition happens via constructor injection (e.g. `ExternalBarangSyncService` depends on `ExternalBarangApiService` and `StockMovementService`). All mutating operations go through `DB::transaction()` blocks, and validation failures surface as `ValidationException` rather than exceptions.
